Anvil Music Player – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Anvil Music Player ("Anvil", "the app") is published by ContortedCrag Studios ("we"). Anvil is built to be private: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. We run no servers for the app and receive nothing about you or your listening.

Data that stays on your device

Anvil reads the music files on your device (through the audio permission you grant) to build your library and play it. Your playlists, play counts, listening statistics, ratings, moods, equalizer settings, rules and preferences are stored only on your device. You can export or restore them yourself with the app's Backup feature; the backup file is saved where you choose and is never sent to us.

Internet requests the app makes

Some optional features contact public third-party services. These requests contain only the information needed for the lookup, and are made directly from your device to the service:

  • Lyrics: track title, artist, album and duration are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to find synced lyrics.
  • Last.fm scrobbling (optional, off by default): only if you sign in to Last.fm in Settings, the title, artist, album, length and time of songs you play are sent to Last.fm so they appear on your Last.fm profile. Your password is sent to Last.fm once to sign in and is never stored by the app. Signing out stops all sending. Last.fm's own privacy policy applies to the data they receive.
  • Cover art and artist images: artist and album names are sent to public services such as Deezer, iTunes Search, MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive to find artwork.

These services receive your IP address as any website would and have their own privacy policies. We do not control them. Features that depend on them are optional, and the app plays your music without them.

Purchases

Anvil Pro and optional "Support the Creator" contributions are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the app only whether a purchase has been made so Pro features can be unlocked. Google's handling of that information is covered by the Google Play privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Audio / media access: to find and play your music.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep music playing with the screen off.
  • Internet: for the optional lookups described above.

Children

Anvil is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Open source

Anvil uses FFmpeg libraries under the LGPL for WMA playback. Source for the modified build is available at github.com/cjwikert/audivra-wma.
